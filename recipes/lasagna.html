Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made of wide, flat pasta sheets layered with fillings like meat sauce, cheese, and vegetables. Originating in the Emilia-Romagna region of Italy, it has become a globally popular comfort food with many regional variations

Ingredients

  • Pasta: Wide, flat sheets of egg or semolina pasta, which can be boiled beforehand or used as oven-ready "no-boil" noodles.
  • Sauce: Traditionally a rich meat ragù (like Bolognese) or a smooth white béchamel sauce, though marinara is widely used in Italian-American versions.
  • Cheese: Classic Italian recipes rely on Parmigiano-Reggiano and mozzarella, while American versions heavily feature ricotta cheese.
  • Fillings: Ground beef, Italian sausage, spinach, mushrooms, zucchini, or roasted eggplant are common additions between the layers.

Steps

  1. Make the Meat Sauce (Ragù)

    Sauté Aromatics. Heat 1 to 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large pot over medium heat, then cook the diced onion, carrot, and celery for 5 to 8 minutes until softened. Stir in the minced garlic for about 30 seconds until fragrant.

    Brown the Meat. Increase the heat to medium-high, add the ground beef (and sausage, if using), and break it apart with a wooden spoon until fully browned. Drain off any excess grease.

    Simmer the Sauce. Stir in the tomato paste for 1 minute, then add the crushed tomatoes, beef broth (or red wine), Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a gentle boil, reduce the heat to low, and simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes until thickened.

  2. Prepare the Pasta and Cheese Filling

    Boil the Noodles.Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook regular lasagna noodles for 1 to 2 minutes less than the package directions until just al dente. Drain them and lay them flat on a lightly oiled baking sheet so they do not stick together. (Skip this step if using oven-ready no-boil sheets).

    Mix the Ricotta Layer. In a medium bowl, stir together the ricotta cheese, beaten egg, grated Parmesan, chopped parsley, and a pinch of salt and pepper until smooth. (If using béchamel instead, melt butter, whisk in flour for 1 minute, gradually whisk in warm milk until thick, and season with salt and nutmeg).

  3. Assemble the Layers

    Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F) and lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish. Repeat the layering sequence until your dish is full:

    • Base Layer. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of the dish so the pasta does not stick.
    • Pasta. Lay 3 to 4 lasagna noodles side-by-side over the sauce, overlapping them slightly to cover the surface.
    • Cheese Filling. Spread one-third of the ricotta mixture evenly over the noodles.
    • Meat & Mozzarella. Spoon one-third of the meat sauce over the ricotta, then sprinkle with a layer of shredded mozzarella.
    • Repeat & Top. Repeat the pasta, ricotta, meat sauce, and mozzarella layers two more times. Finish with a final layer of noodles topped with the remaining meat sauce, mozzarella, and extra Parmesan.
  4. Bake and Rest

    Bake Covered. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil, spraying the underside with cooking spray so it does not stick to the cheese. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes until heated through.

    Brown the Top. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 15 to 25 minutes until the cheese is melted, golden brown, and bubbling around the edges.

    Rest Before Slicing. Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it sit on the counter for 15 to 20 minutes before cutting. Resting allows the hot layers to firm up so the slices hold their shape cleanly.
